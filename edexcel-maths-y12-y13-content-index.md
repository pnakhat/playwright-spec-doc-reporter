# Edexcel International A Level — Mathematics & Further Mathematics

## High-Level Content Index (Y12 / Y13)

This is a high-level index of the content areas for the Edexcel (Pearson)
**International Advanced Level (IAL)** in Mathematics and Further Mathematics
(2018 specification, first teaching September 2018). It is organised by
qualification and unit, listing the main topic areas within each unit.

- **A Level Mathematics** is built from six units: **P1, P2, P3, P4** plus two
  applied units — here **M1** and **S1**.
- **A Level Further Mathematics** is built from six further units: three Further
  Pure units **FP1, FP2, FP3** plus applied units — here **D1, M2, S2**.

> **Year split (typical).** Y12 (International AS) usually covers **P1, P2** and
> one applied unit; Y13 (International A2) covers **P3, P4** and a second applied
> unit. For Further Maths, FP1 and lower-numbered applied units usually sit in
> Y12, with FP2/FP3 and higher applied units in Y13. Exact sequencing varies by
> centre.

## Part 1 — Mathematics ("normal maths")

### P1 — Pure Mathematics 1
*Aligned to the Pearson Edexcel IAL Pure Mathematics 1 student book (9 chapters).*

1. **Algebraic expressions** — index laws; expanding brackets; factorising; negative and fractional indices; surds; rationalising denominators
2. **Quadratics** — solving quadratic equations; completing the square; functions; quadratic graphs; the discriminant
3. **Equations and inequalities** — linear simultaneous equations; quadratic simultaneous equations; simultaneous equations on graphs; linear inequalities; quadratic inequalities; inequalities on graphs; regions
4. **Graphs and transformations** — cubic graphs; reciprocal graphs; points of intersection; translating graphs; stretching graphs; transforming functions
5. **Straight line graphs** — y = mx + c; equations of straight lines; parallel and perpendicular lines; length and area
6. **Trigonometric ratios** — the cosine rule; the sine rule; areas of triangles; solving triangle problems; graphs of sine, cosine and tangent; transforming trigonometric graphs
7. **Radians** — radian measure; arc length; areas of sectors and segments
8. **Differentiation** — gradients of curves; finding the derivative; differentiating xⁿ; differentiating quadratics; functions with two or more terms; gradients, tangents and normals; second order derivatives
9. **Integration** — integrating xⁿ; indefinite integrals; finding functions

### P2 — Pure Mathematics 2
- Proof
- Algebra and functions (algebraic division, factor/remainder theorem, partial fractions, modulus, composite and inverse functions)
- Coordinate geometry in the (x, y) plane
- Sequences and series (arithmetic and geometric series, binomial expansion)
- Exponentials and logarithms
- Trigonometry (radians, arcs and sectors, further identities)
- Differentiation
- Integration (trapezium rule, further techniques)

### P3 — Pure Mathematics 3
- Algebra and functions (further partial fractions, functions and their graphs)
- Trigonometry (compound and double angle formulae, R cos/sin form)
- Exponentials and logarithms
- Differentiation (product, quotient and chain rules; implicit and parametric)
- Integration (by substitution and by parts, standard integrals)
- Numerical methods (location of roots, iteration, Newton–Raphson)

### P4 — Pure Mathematics 4
- Proof
- Algebra and functions (partial fractions applied to series)
- Coordinate geometry in the (x, y) plane (parametric equations)
- Binomial expansion (for any rational index)
- Differentiation (rates of change, differential equations set-up)
- Integration (further techniques, volumes of revolution, differential equations)
- Vectors (vector geometry in two and three dimensions, lines)

### M1 — Mechanics 1
- Mathematical models in mechanics
- Vectors in mechanics
- Kinematics of a particle moving in a straight line (constant acceleration, motion graphs)
- Dynamics of a particle moving in a straight line (Newton's laws, connected particles)
- Statics of a particle (equilibrium, friction)
- Moments

### S1 — Statistics 1
- Mathematical models in probability and statistics
- Representation and summary of data (measures of location and spread)
- Probability (events, conditional probability, tree/Venn diagrams)
- Correlation and regression
- Discrete random variables (probability distributions, expectation, variance)
- The Normal distribution

## Part 2 — Further Mathematics

### FP1 — Further Pure Mathematics 1
- Complex numbers (arithmetic, Argand diagrams, modulus–argument form)
- Roots of quadratic equations
- Numerical solution of equations (interval bisection, linear interpolation, Newton–Raphson)
- Coordinate systems (the parabola and the rectangular hyperbola)
- Matrix algebra
- Transformations using matrices
- Series (standard summation formulae)
- Proof by mathematical induction

### FP2 — Further Pure Mathematics 2
- Inequalities
- Series (method of differences)
- Further complex numbers (de Moivre's theorem, nth roots, loci)
- First order differential equations
- Second order differential equations
- Maclaurin and Taylor series
- Polar coordinates

### FP3 — Further Pure Mathematics 3
- Hyperbolic functions
- Further coordinate systems (the ellipse and the hyperbola)
- Differentiation (of inverse trig and hyperbolic functions)
- Integration (reduction formulae, arc length, surface area)
- Vectors (vector product, scalar triple product, lines and planes)
- Further matrix algebra (determinants, inverses, eigenvalues and eigenvectors)

### D1 — Decision Mathematics 1
- Algorithms (and their implementation, sorting and bin-packing)
- Algorithms on graphs (minimum spanning tree — Kruskal's and Prim's; Dijkstra's shortest path)
- Route inspection (the Chinese postman problem)
- Critical path analysis
- Linear programming (graphical methods)
- Matchings (bipartite graphs, maximum matching algorithm)

### M2 — Mechanics 2
- Kinematics of a particle moving in a straight line or plane (variable acceleration, projectiles)
- Centres of mass
- Work, energy and power
- Collisions (impulse and momentum, coefficient of restitution)
- Statics of rigid bodies

### S2 — Statistics 2
- The Binomial distribution
- The Poisson distribution
- Continuous random variables (probability density and cumulative distribution functions)
- Continuous distributions (the continuous uniform / rectangular distribution)
- Hypothesis testing (including significance tests and sampling)
