# Edexcel International A Level — Mathematics & Further Mathematics

## Content Index (Y12 / Y13)

A chapter-level index of the Edexcel (Pearson) **International Advanced Level (IAL)**
in Mathematics and Further Mathematics (2018 specification), organised by unit and
aligned to the **Pearson IAL student-book** series used at international schools such
as Cranleigh Abu Dhabi.

- **A Level Mathematics** = **P1, P2, P3, P4** + two applied units — here **M1** and **S1**.
- **A Level Further Mathematics** = **FP1, FP2, FP3** + applied units — here **D1, M2, S2**.

> **Year split (typical).** Y12 (International AS) usually covers **P1, P2** and one
> applied unit; Y13 (International A2) covers **P3, P4** and a second applied unit.
> For Further Maths, **FP1** and lower-numbered applied units sit in Y12, with
> **FP2/FP3** and higher applied units in Y13. Exact sequencing varies by centre.

> **Accuracy note.** **P1** is verified chapter-by-chapter against the actual book
> contents page. For the other units, chapter titles follow the Pearson IAL student
> books and the bullets list the key content within each chapter — spot-check the
> fine detail against each book's contents page (the source PDFs were not directly
> reachable from this environment).

## Part 1 — Mathematics ("normal maths")

### P1 — Pure Mathematics 1
*Verified against the Pearson IAL Pure Mathematics 1 student book (9 chapters).*

1. **Algebraic expressions** — index laws; expanding brackets; factorising; negative and fractional indices; surds; rationalising denominators
2. **Quadratics** — solving quadratic equations; completing the square; functions; quadratic graphs; the discriminant
3. **Equations and inequalities** — linear simultaneous equations; quadratic simultaneous equations; simultaneous equations on graphs; linear inequalities; quadratic inequalities; inequalities on graphs; regions
4. **Graphs and transformations** — cubic graphs; reciprocal graphs; points of intersection; translating graphs; stretching graphs; transforming functions
5. **Straight line graphs** — y = mx + c; equations of straight lines; parallel and perpendicular lines; length and area
6. **Trigonometric ratios** — the cosine rule; the sine rule; areas of triangles; solving triangle problems; graphs of sine, cosine and tangent; transforming trigonometric graphs
7. **Radians** — radian measure; arc length; areas of sectors and segments
8. **Differentiation** — gradients of curves; finding the derivative; differentiating xⁿ; differentiating quadratics; functions with two or more terms; gradients, tangents and normals; second order derivatives
9. **Integration** — integrating xⁿ; indefinite integrals; finding functions

### P2 — Pure Mathematics 2
1. **Algebraic methods** — proof (deduction, exhaustion, disproof by counter-example); dividing polynomials; the factor theorem
2. **Coordinate geometry in the (x, y) plane** — midpoints and perpendicular bisectors; equation of a circle; intersections of lines and circles; tangent and chord properties; circles and triangles
3. **Exponentials and logarithms** — exponential functions; y = eˣ; exponential modelling; logarithms; laws of logarithms; solving equations using logs; natural logarithms; non-linear data
4. **The binomial expansion** — Pascal's triangle; factorial notation; the binomial expansion; solving binomial problems; binomial estimation
5. **Sequences and series** — arithmetic sequences and series; geometric sequences and series; sum to infinity; sigma notation; recurrence relations; modelling with series
6. **Trigonometric identities and equations** — angles in all four quadrants; exact values; trig identities; simple and harder trig equations; equations and identities
7. **Differentiation** — increasing and decreasing functions; stationary points; sketching gradient functions; modelling with differentiation
8. **Integration** — definite integrals; area under a curve; area between curves and lines; the trapezium rule

### P3 — Pure Mathematics 3
1. **Algebraic methods** — partial fractions (proper and improper fractions); algebraic division
2. **Functions and graphs** — the modulus function; functions and mappings; composite functions; inverse functions; transformations y = |f(x)| and y = f(|x|)
3. **Trigonometric functions** — secant, cosecant and cotangent; identities using sec/cosec/cot; inverse trigonometric functions
4. **Trigonometric addition formulae** — addition formulae; double-angle formulae; solving equations; the R cos(θ ± α) / R sin(θ ± α) form; proving identities
5. **Exponentials and logarithms** — eˣ and ln x; derivatives; exponential modelling and growth/decay
6. **Differentiation** — chain, product and quotient rules; differentiating eˣ, ln x and trig functions; implicit and parametric differentiation; rates of change
7. **Integration** — standard integrals; integration by substitution; integration by parts; using partial fractions; areas and the trapezium rule
8. **Numerical methods** — locating roots; iteration; the Newton–Raphson method

### P4 — Pure Mathematics 4
1. **Proof** — proof by contradiction
2. **Partial fractions** — into partial fractions; improper fractions; applications to series/binomial
3. **Coordinate geometry in the (x, y) plane** — parametric equations; converting to Cartesian; parametric curves; points of intersection
4. **Binomial expansion** — expansion for any rational index; using partial fractions; approximations and validity
5. **Differentiation** — differentiating parametric equations; connected rates of change; forming simple differential equations
6. **Integration** — further techniques; volumes of revolution; solving differential equations (separating variables)
7. **Vectors** — 3D coordinates; vectors in three dimensions; the equation of a line in 3D; points of intersection; the scalar (dot) product; angles between lines

### M1 — Mechanics 1
1. **Mathematical models in mechanics** — modelling assumptions; quantities and units (S.I.)
2. **Constant acceleration** — displacement–time and velocity–time graphs; the constant-acceleration (suvat) formulae; vertical motion under gravity
3. **Vectors in mechanics** — vectors in kinematics; i, j notation; velocity, acceleration and force as vectors
4. **Dynamics of a particle moving in a straight line** — force diagrams; Newton's laws of motion; motion in two dimensions; connected particles and pulleys
5. **Forces and friction** — resolving forces; inclined planes; friction and the coefficient of friction
6. **Momentum and impulse** — momentum and impulse; conservation of linear momentum; direct impact/collisions
7. **Statics of a particle** — equilibrium of a particle; forces in equilibrium; static problems with friction
8. **Moments** — moment of a force; resultant moments; equilibrium of rigid bodies; tilting and non-uniform rods

### S1 — Statistics 1
1. **Mathematical models in probability and statistics** — the modelling process
2. **Measures of location and spread** — mean, median, mode; quartiles and percentiles; variance and standard deviation; coding
3. **Representation of data** — histograms; box plots; outliers; skewness; comparing distributions
4. **Probability** — sample spaces; Venn diagrams; mutually exclusive and independent events; conditional probability; tree diagrams
5. **Correlation** — scatter diagrams; the product moment correlation coefficient; interpreting correlation
6. **Regression** — the least-squares regression line; interpolation and extrapolation
7. **Discrete random variables** — probability distributions; the cumulative distribution function; expectation E(X); variance Var(X)
8. **The Normal distribution** — the standard normal distribution; finding probabilities; finding unknown means/standard deviations

## Part 2 — Further Mathematics

### FP1 — Further Pure Mathematics 1
1. **Complex numbers** — imaginary and complex numbers; complex arithmetic; complex conjugates; Argand diagrams; modulus and argument; modulus–argument form
2. **Roots of quadratic equations** — roots of a quadratic with complex/real coefficients; sums and products of roots; forming equations
3. **Numerical solutions of equations** — locating roots; interval bisection; linear interpolation; the Newton–Raphson method
4. **Coordinate systems** — the parabola and the rectangular hyperbola; parametric forms; tangents and normals; loci
5. **Matrices** — matrix operations; matrix multiplication; determinants; the inverse of a 2 × 2 matrix
6. **Transformations using matrices** — linear transformations in two dimensions; reflections and rotations; enlargements and stretches; successive transformations
7. **Series** — standard summation results (Σr, Σr², Σr³); summing series
8. **Proof by induction** — mathematical induction; divisibility results; recurrence relations; matrix statements

### FP2 — Further Pure Mathematics 2
1. **Inequalities** — algebraic inequalities; inequalities involving the modulus; graphical methods
2. **Series** — the method of differences
3. **Further complex numbers** — de Moivre's theorem; trigonometric identities; sums of series; nth roots of a complex number; loci and regions in the Argand diagram; the exponential form re^{iθ}
4. **First order differential equations** — separating variables; families of solutions; exact equations; the integrating factor; first-order linear equations; reducible equations
5. **Second order differential equations** — homogeneous equations (the auxiliary equation); non-homogeneous equations (the particular integral); boundary conditions; modelling with 2nd-order equations
6. **Maclaurin and Taylor series** — Maclaurin series; Taylor series; series solutions of differential equations
7. **Polar coordinates** — polar and Cartesian coordinates; sketching polar curves; area enclosed by a polar curve; tangents to polar curves

### FP3 — Further Pure Mathematics 3
1. **Hyperbolic functions** — definitions and graphs; inverse hyperbolic functions; hyperbolic identities; solving equations
2. **Further coordinate systems** — the ellipse and the hyperbola; eccentricity; foci and directrices; tangents and normals; loci
3. **Differentiation** — differentiating inverse trigonometric and hyperbolic functions
4. **Integration** — integrating inverse trig/hyperbolic forms; standard results; reduction formulae; arc length; area of a surface of revolution
5. **Vectors** — the vector (cross) product; the scalar triple product; equations of lines and planes; distances and angles
6. **Further matrix algebra** — 3 × 3 determinants and inverses; solving systems of linear equations; eigenvalues and eigenvectors; diagonalisation

### D1 — Decision Mathematics 1
1. **Algorithms** — using and understanding algorithms; flow charts; sorting algorithms (bubble sort, quick sort); bin-packing algorithms; order of an algorithm
2. **Graphs and networks** — modelling with graphs; graph terminology; special graphs; representing graphs (adjacency matrices)
3. **Algorithms on graphs** — minimum spanning trees: Kruskal's and Prim's algorithms (on a network and from a matrix); Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm
4. **Route inspection** — the route inspection (Chinese postman) problem; Eulerian and semi-Eulerian graphs
5. **Critical path analysis** — precedence tables; activity networks; dummy activities; early and late event times; critical activities and float; Gantt (cascade) charts; scheduling
6. **Linear programming** — formulating LP problems; graphical solutions; objective lines and the vertex method; integer solutions
7. **Matchings** — bipartite graphs; matchings; alternating paths; the maximum matching algorithm

### M2 — Mechanics 2
1. **Kinematics** — variable acceleration; using calculus for kinematics; motion in two dimensions with vectors; **projectiles** (horizontal and vertical components, projectile formulae)
2. **Centres of mass** — centre of mass of a system of particles; of uniform laminae and standard shapes; of frameworks; equilibrium of a suspended lamina
3. **Work, energy and power** — work done by a force; kinetic and potential energy; the work–energy principle; power
4. **Collisions** — momentum and impulse; conservation of momentum; Newton's law of restitution; direct impact of particles; impact with a surface; successive impacts
5. **Statics of rigid bodies** — moments and resultant moments; equilibrium of rigid bodies; rigid bodies with friction

### S2 — Statistics 2
1. **The binomial distribution** — the binomial model; using tables; mean and variance
2. **The Poisson distribution** — the Poisson model; using tables; mean and variance; the Poisson approximation to the binomial
3. **Continuous random variables** — the probability density function (p.d.f.); the cumulative distribution function (c.d.f.); mean, variance, mode and median
4. **Continuous distributions** — the continuous uniform (rectangular) distribution; the normal approximation to the binomial and Poisson distributions
5. **Hypothesis testing** — populations and sampling; test statistics; hypothesis tests for a binomial proportion and a Poisson mean; critical regions and significance levels
