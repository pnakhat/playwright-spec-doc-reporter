Edexcel International A Level

Mathematics & Further Mathematics — Detailed Content Index (Y12 / Y13)

A chapter- and sub-section–level index of the Edexcel (Pearson) International Advanced Level (IAL) in Mathematics and Further Mathematics (2018 specification), aligned to the Pearson IAL student-book series used at international schools such as Cranleigh Abu Dhabi.

A Level Mathematics = P1, P2, P3, P4 + two applied units (here M1, S1).  •  A Level Further Mathematics = FP1, FP2, FP3 + applied units (here D1, M2, S2).

Year split (typical). Y12 (International AS) usually covers P1, P2 and one applied unit; Y13 (International A2) covers P3, P4 and a second applied unit. For Further Maths, FP1 and lower-numbered applied units sit in Y12, with FP2/FP3 and higher applied units in Y13. Exact sequencing varies by centre.
Accuracy note. P1 sub-sections are exact (from the book contents page). For the other 11 units the chapters and sub-sections are reconstructed from the Pearson IAL student books — accurate in coverage, but validate the exact sub-section numbering against each book's contents page.
