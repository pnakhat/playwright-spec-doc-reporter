Edexcel International A Level

Mathematics & Further Mathematics — Content Index (Y12 / Y13)

A chapter-level index of the Edexcel (Pearson) International Advanced Level (IAL) in Mathematics and Further Mathematics (2018 specification), aligned to the Pearson IAL student-book series used at international schools such as Cranleigh Abu Dhabi.

A Level Mathematics = P1, P2, P3, P4 + two applied units (here M1, S1).  •  A Level Further Mathematics = FP1, FP2, FP3 + applied units (here D1, M2, S2).

Year split (typical). Y12 (International AS) usually covers P1, P2 and one applied unit; Y13 (International A2) covers P3, P4 and a second applied unit. For Further Maths, FP1 and lower-numbered applied units sit in Y12, with FP2/FP3 and higher applied units in Y13. Exact sequencing varies by centre.
Accuracy note. P1 is verified chapter-by-chapter against the actual book contents page. For the other units, chapter titles follow the Pearson IAL student books and the bullets list the key content within each chapter — spot-check the fine detail against each book's contents page.

Part 1 — Mathematics ("normal maths")

P1 — Pure Mathematics 1 VERIFIED

  1. Algebraic expressions — index laws; expanding brackets; factorising; negative and fractional indices; surds; rationalising denominators
  2. Quadratics — solving quadratic equations; completing the square; functions; quadratic graphs; the discriminant
  3. Equations and inequalities — linear & quadratic simultaneous equations; simultaneous equations on graphs; linear & quadratic inequalities; inequalities on graphs; regions
  4. Graphs and transformations — cubic graphs; reciprocal graphs; points of intersection; translating graphs; stretching graphs; transforming functions
  5. Straight line graphs — y = mx + c; equations of straight lines; parallel and perpendicular lines; length and area
  6. Trigonometric ratios — cosine rule; sine rule; areas of triangles; solving triangle problems; graphs of sin, cos, tan; transforming trig graphs
  7. Radians — radian measure; arc length; areas of sectors and segments
  8. Differentiation — gradients of curves; finding the derivative; differentiating xⁿ and quadratics; multi-term functions; tangents and normals; second order derivatives
  9. Integration — integrating xⁿ; indefinite integrals; finding functions

P2 — Pure Mathematics 2

  1. Algebraic methods — proof (deduction, exhaustion, counter-example); dividing polynomials; the factor theorem
  2. Coordinate geometry in the (x, y) plane — midpoints and perpendicular bisectors; equation of a circle; intersections of lines and circles; tangent and chord properties; circles and triangles
  3. Exponentials and logarithms — exponential functions; y = eˣ; exponential modelling; logarithms; laws of logs; solving equations with logs; natural logarithms; non-linear data
  4. The binomial expansion — Pascal's triangle; factorial notation; the binomial expansion; solving problems; binomial estimation
  5. Sequences and series — arithmetic and geometric sequences and series; sum to infinity; sigma notation; recurrence relations; modelling
  6. Trigonometric identities and equations — angles in four quadrants; exact values; trig identities; simple and harder trig equations
  7. Differentiation — increasing/decreasing functions; stationary points; sketching gradient functions; modelling
  8. Integration — definite integrals; area under a curve; area between curves and lines; the trapezium rule

P3 — Pure Mathematics 3

  1. Algebraic methods — partial fractions (proper and improper); algebraic division
  2. Functions and graphs — the modulus function; functions and mappings; composite functions; inverse functions; transformations y = |f(x)| and y = f(|x|)
  3. Trigonometric functions — secant, cosecant, cotangent; identities; inverse trigonometric functions
  4. Trigonometric addition formulae — addition and double-angle formulae; the R cos(θ ± α) form; solving equations; proving identities
  5. Exponentials and logarithms — eˣ and ln x; derivatives; exponential modelling, growth and decay
  6. Differentiation — chain, product and quotient rules; eˣ, ln x and trig functions; implicit and parametric differentiation; rates of change
  7. Integration — standard integrals; substitution; by parts; using partial fractions; areas; trapezium rule
  8. Numerical methods — locating roots; iteration; the Newton–Raphson method

P4 — Pure Mathematics 4

  1. Proof — proof by contradiction
  2. Partial fractions — into partial fractions; improper fractions; applications
  3. Coordinate geometry in the (x, y) plane — parametric equations; converting to Cartesian; parametric curves; points of intersection
  4. Binomial expansion — expansion for any rational index; using partial fractions; approximations and validity
  5. Differentiation — parametric differentiation; connected rates of change; forming differential equations
  6. Integration — further techniques; volumes of revolution; solving differential equations (separating variables)
  7. Vectors — 3D coordinates; vectors in three dimensions; equation of a line in 3D; points of intersection; the scalar (dot) product; angles

M1 — Mechanics 1

  1. Mathematical models in mechanics — modelling assumptions; quantities and S.I. units
  2. Constant acceleration — displacement–time and velocity–time graphs; the suvat formulae; vertical motion under gravity
  3. Vectors in mechanics — i, j notation; velocity, acceleration and force as vectors
  4. Dynamics of a particle moving in a straight line — force diagrams; Newton's laws; motion in two dimensions; connected particles and pulleys
  5. Forces and friction — resolving forces; inclined planes; friction and the coefficient of friction
  6. Momentum and impulse — momentum and impulse; conservation of linear momentum; direct impact/collisions
  7. Statics of a particle — equilibrium of a particle; forces in equilibrium; statics with friction
  8. Moments — moment of a force; resultant moments; equilibrium of rigid bodies; tilting and non-uniform rods

S1 — Statistics 1

  1. Mathematical models in probability and statistics — the modelling process
  2. Measures of location and spread — mean, median, mode; quartiles and percentiles; variance and standard deviation; coding
  3. Representation of data — histograms; box plots; outliers; skewness; comparing distributions
  4. Probability — sample spaces; Venn diagrams; mutually exclusive and independent events; conditional probability; tree diagrams
  5. Correlation — scatter diagrams; the product moment correlation coefficient; interpretation
  6. Regression — the least-squares regression line; interpolation and extrapolation
  7. Discrete random variables — probability distributions; the cumulative distribution function; E(X); Var(X)
  8. The Normal distribution — the standard normal distribution; finding probabilities; finding unknown means/standard deviations

Part 2 — Further Mathematics

FP1 — Further Pure Mathematics 1

  1. Complex numbers — imaginary and complex numbers; complex arithmetic; conjugates; Argand diagrams; modulus and argument; modulus–argument form
  2. Roots of quadratic equations — roots with real/complex coefficients; sums and products of roots; forming equations
  3. Numerical solutions of equations — locating roots; interval bisection; linear interpolation; Newton–Raphson
  4. Coordinate systems — the parabola and rectangular hyperbola; parametric forms; tangents and normals; loci
  5. Matrices — matrix operations; multiplication; determinants; inverse of a 2 × 2 matrix
  6. Transformations using matrices — linear transformations; reflections and rotations; enlargements and stretches; successive transformations
  7. Series — standard summation results (Σr, Σr², Σr³); summing series
  8. Proof by induction — mathematical induction; divisibility; recurrence relations; matrix statements

FP2 — Further Pure Mathematics 2

  1. Inequalities — algebraic inequalities; modulus inequalities; graphical methods
  2. Series — the method of differences
  3. Further complex numbers — de Moivre's theorem; trig identities; sums of series; nth roots; loci and regions; exponential form re^{iθ}
  4. First order differential equations — separating variables; exact equations; the integrating factor; first-order linear; reducible equations
  5. Second order differential equations — homogeneous (auxiliary equation); non-homogeneous (particular integral); boundary conditions; modelling
  6. Maclaurin and Taylor series — Maclaurin series; Taylor series; series solutions of differential equations
  7. Polar coordinates — polar and Cartesian coordinates; sketching curves; area enclosed by a polar curve; tangents

FP3 — Further Pure Mathematics 3

  1. Hyperbolic functions — definitions and graphs; inverse hyperbolic functions; identities; solving equations
  2. Further coordinate systems — the ellipse and hyperbola; eccentricity; foci and directrices; tangents and normals; loci
  3. Differentiation — differentiating inverse trigonometric and hyperbolic functions
  4. Integration — inverse trig/hyperbolic forms; standard results; reduction formulae; arc length; surface of revolution
  5. Vectors — the vector (cross) product; scalar triple product; equations of lines and planes; distances and angles
  6. Further matrix algebra — 3 × 3 determinants and inverses; systems of linear equations; eigenvalues and eigenvectors; diagonalisation

D1 — Decision Mathematics 1

  1. Algorithms — using and understanding algorithms; flow charts; sorting (bubble, quick sort); bin-packing; order of an algorithm
  2. Graphs and networks — modelling with graphs; terminology; special graphs; adjacency matrices
  3. Algorithms on graphs — minimum spanning trees (Kruskal's, Prim's); Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm
  4. Route inspection — the route inspection (Chinese postman) problem; Eulerian graphs
  5. Critical path analysis — precedence tables; activity networks; dummy activities; early/late event times; critical activities and float; Gantt charts; scheduling
  6. Linear programming — formulating LP problems; graphical solutions; objective lines and the vertex method; integer solutions
  7. Matchings — bipartite graphs; matchings; alternating paths; the maximum matching algorithm

M2 — Mechanics 2

  1. Kinematics — variable acceleration; calculus for kinematics; motion in two dimensions; projectiles (horizontal/vertical components, projectile formulae)
  2. Centres of mass — systems of particles; uniform laminae and standard shapes; frameworks; equilibrium of a suspended lamina
  3. Work, energy and power — work done by a force; kinetic and potential energy; the work–energy principle; power
  4. Collisions — momentum and impulse; conservation of momentum; Newton's law of restitution; direct impact; impact with a surface; successive impacts
  5. Statics of rigid bodies — moments and resultant moments; equilibrium of rigid bodies; friction

S2 — Statistics 2

  1. The binomial distribution — the binomial model; using tables; mean and variance
  2. The Poisson distribution — the Poisson model; using tables; mean and variance; Poisson approximation to the binomial
  3. Continuous random variables — the probability density function; the cumulative distribution function; mean, variance, mode and median
  4. Continuous distributions — the continuous uniform (rectangular) distribution; normal approximation to the binomial and Poisson
  5. Hypothesis testing — populations and sampling; test statistics; tests for a binomial proportion and a Poisson mean; critical regions and significance levels
